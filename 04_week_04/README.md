# Week 4 — Electronics and Amplifiers

## Objective
Develop from basic components into transistor switching and small-signal amplification using calculations and LTspice.

## Contents
- `01_Notes/Week_04_Notes.md` — capacitors, diodes, BJTs and common-emitter amplifiers.
- `02_Calculations/Week_04_Calculations.md` — design calculations and checks.
- `03_Circuit_Designs/Circuit_Descriptions.md` — circuit topology descriptions.
- `04_LTspice_Simulations/Simulation_Setup.md` — simulation workflow and limitations.
- `05_Results_and_Analysis/Week_04_Results.md` — recorded results and interpretation.

## Main outcomes
1. Explain RC charging/discharging and time constant.
2. Explain diode bias and LED current limiting.
3. Use an NPN BJT as a low-side switch.
4. Establish a voltage-divider bias point for a common-emitter amplifier.
5. Measure transient gain and identify phase inversion.
6. Distinguish transient measurements from a verified frequency response.

## Tools
LTspice, hand calculations and component datasheets.

## Important limitation
The transient common-emitter measurement recorded a gain magnitude near 2. A separate AC-analysis plot reported near 28.91 dB and phase near 78.9°, but it was not reconciled with the transient result. It is marked **unverified**.
